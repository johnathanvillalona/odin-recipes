Burrito Bowl

About The Recipe

A quick bowl made with rice, chicken, beans, and a few fresh toppings. It's filling, healthy, and takes about 15 minutes to make.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 1 cooked chicken breast, diced
  • 1 can black beans, drained
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • Sour cream
  • Taco seasoning

Steps

  1. Heat the chicken in a pan and sprinkle with taco seasoning.
  2. Warm the black beans in the microwave.
  3. Put the rice in a bowl.
  4. Add the chicken and beans.
  5. Top with avocado and a spoonful of sour cream.
  6. Enjoy!

Burrito Bowl

About The Recipe

This burrito bowl is a fresh, protein-packed meal with seasoned chicken served over rice and topped with black beans, lettuce, avocado, corn salsa, pico de gallo, and a dollop of sour cream. It has a great mix of warm and cold ingredients, creamy textures, and bright flavors, similar to what you'd get at a fast-casual restaurant.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast, diced
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 8 small flour or corn tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup shredded cheese (cheddar or Mexican blend)
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • Sour cream (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat a skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add the diced chicken and cook for 6 to 8 minutes until no longer pink.
  3. Sprinkle the taco seasoning over the chicken and add 2 tablespoons of water.
  4. Stir and cook for 2 more minutes.
  5. Warm the tortillas in the microwave for 20 seconds.
  6. Fill each tortilla with chicken.
  7. Top with lettuce, cheese, tomato, and sour cream if desired.
